# 🚗💨 DRIVE MAD

> **Drive fast. Stay balanced. Don't flip. Reach the finish. 🏁**

**Drive Mad** is a browser-based physics driving game where you control a vehicle across crazy terrain, ramps, bumps and gaps.

Your mission is simple:

```text
🚗 START
   ↓
💨 ACCELERATE
   ↓
🏔️ CROSS THE TERRAIN
   ↓
🌀 CONTROL THE CAR
   ↓
⚖️ KEEP YOUR BALANCE
   ↓
🏁 REACH THE FINISH
